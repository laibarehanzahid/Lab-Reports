
# W15e - Isotherms of Real Gases

## Overview
This experiment investigates the thermodynamic behaviour of sulfur hexafluoride (SF6) under different pressure and temperature conditions.

The objective is to study real-gas isotherms, liquid-vapour phase transitions, and the critical region.

## Methods
- Pressure-volume measurements at different temperatures
- Visualization of experimental isotherms using Python
- Identification of liquid-vapour coexistence regions
- Determination of saturation vapour pressure
- Clausius-Clapeyron analysis
- Regression and uncertainty analysis
