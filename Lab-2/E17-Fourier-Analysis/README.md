# E17e - Fourier Analysis of Coupled Electric Oscillations

## Overview
This experiment investigates coupled electrical oscillations
in capacitively and inductively coupled LC circuits.

The objective is to identify normal-mode frequencies, study
beat oscillations, and determine coupling factors.

## Methods
- Voltage measurements using an oscilloscope
- Fourier analysis of oscillatory signals
- Frequency-spectrum analysis and peak detection
- Identification of normal modes
- Determination of capacitive and inductive coupling factors
- Curve fitting and uncertainty analysis
- Investigation of mutual inductance
